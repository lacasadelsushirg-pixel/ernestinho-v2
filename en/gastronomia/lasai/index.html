Ernestinho Carioca · Humaitá

Lasai

Rafa Costa e Silva’s cuisine works with modern techniques, garden vegetables and Brazilian produce.

Fine diningContemporary BrazilianVegetablesPremium

Visual identity and atmosphere

Lasai concentrates the experience around one large table and an open kitchen where the team’s work remains visible. Photographs show a contained space, few diners and precisely constructed dishes. It does not operate like a conventional dining room: proximity to the fire, chef and other guests makes every movement part of dinner.

History and concept

Rafa Costa e Silva and Malena Cardiel developed Lasai around contemporary techniques, Brazilian produce and direct relationships with their own gardens and small farmers. The current restaurant at Largo dos Leões works exclusively with a tasting menu and reservations. Vegetables occupy a central place without excluding fish or meat.

My personal take as Ernestinho

For me, Lasai is not a recommendation for casually “going out to eat” on any night; it is a travel decision centered on a kitchen. I would include it for anyone wanting to observe processes, accept a defined menu and engage with the sequence of dishes. I would not choose it if you need the freedom to choose from an à-la-carte menu, a quick dinner or a setting where the group can move without following the kitchen’s rhythm.

What to order and why

It would be wrong to invent a list of fixed dishes here: the menu changes, and the main choice is accepting the tasting proposal. Public references describe a sequence of snacks, courses and desserts, but the current composition should be checked when booking. If there are restrictions or preferences, I would communicate them before payment and ask about available pairings.

Experience by time of day

Lasai operates as an evening experience with precise arrival times. On weekdays, dinner begins at a defined hour; on Saturdays there may be two seatings, according to the official system. Arriving late affects a shared sequence, so I would reserve the entire evening and avoid scheduling another activity afterward.

My specific advice

I would read the cancellation conditions and report allergies before confirming. I would choose the experience only if every companion accepts the menu and time it requires. The mistake would be booking for prestige without enjoying the logic of a small table where attention, conversation and cooking happen only meters away.

Lasai requires planning: before booking, check the menu, duration and cancellation rules. Communicate allergies and restrictions early and confirm which accommodations are possible.

What to combine nearby

Beforehand, I would walk through Botafogo or Humaitá and have something light without turning the afternoon into another meal. Largo dos Leões can be combined with Cobal do Humaitá or an early visit to Casa Firjan. After dinner, I would return directly to the accommodation and preserve the experience as the day’s conclusion.

For a multi-course dinner, avoid another fixed-time evening activity. Leave a margin for arrival, enjoy the full service and arrange transport home.
