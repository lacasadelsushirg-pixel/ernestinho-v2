Kilograma Centro

A flexible Rua Riachuelo buffet for a practical meal in Central Rio.

Downtown

Identity and atmosphere

Kilograma on Rua Riachuelo works better as a convenient meal stop than a dining destination. Sources describe salads and hot dishes, while some also mention grilling, sushi, pizza or rodízio. Those descriptions are inconsistent, so I would not promise every station is open at all times. Look at the actual selection and decide if it suits your meal.

History and concept

The address is listed as either 313 or 315 Rua Riachuelo, sometimes with different shop letters; a menu on kilograma.shop lists number 315 and phone (21) 2146-2666. I would check the latest map pin and storefront, as there may be more than one entrance. It is a practical stop between downtown and Lapa.

Menus and hours may change; check before your visit.

My personal take as Ernestinho

Before serving myself, I would look over the buffet, ask the price per kilogram and check what has just been replenished. Then I would take small portions. For pizza, sushi or grilled dishes, confirm whether those stations operate and whether they are charged by weight, fixed-price rodízio or separately. The sources do not establish one format for every time.

What to order and why

Reviews are mixed. Some customers value the variety and convenience; others have complained of food they perceived as cold, dry or less fresh, and inconsistent service. That does not establish how every meal will be, but it supports checking temperature, replenishment and appearance before filling a plate. If one station does not appeal, ask staff or choose another.

Experience by time of day

I did not find a verified current per-kilo price or unambiguous hours. Waze and other directories show different time ranges. I therefore would not promise low prices or regular nighttime service. Check whether it is open, which service format operates and what it costs. Drinks, desserts and certain dishes may be extra; ask about rules for children and groups.

My specific advice

My advice is to use Kilograma when you are already downtown and need variety. Buffets can involve shared serving utensils and cross-contact; anyone with allergies should speak with staff. Do not rely only on a generic 'vegetarian' label without checking sauces and stocks. If price matters, read the price board first: weight adds up quickly.

At Kilograma Centro, check whether lunch is charged by weight or another format and look over the buffet before serving yourself. Start with a moderate portion to make better choices and control spending.

What to combine nearby

Rua Riachuelo connects Central Rio and Lapa and can fit into a cultural day depending on schedule. Metro, bus, taxi, or ride-hailing can get you close, but the best route depends on the exact point and time. If you are continuing to Santa Teresa or a timed visit, leave enough lunch margin. Its strength is flexibility; its limitation is precisely the lack of consistent official information on price, hours, and formats.

It is a convenient stop on a day of appointments or sightseeing downtown. Check opening hours, particularly on weekends and public holidays when local businesses may operate differently.
