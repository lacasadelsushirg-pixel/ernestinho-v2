Eclipse

A corner bar-restaurant blending pizza, feijoada, television screens and piano with late-night service.

24 hoursBarPizzasBrazilianLate nightMusic

Identity and atmosphere

Eclipse occupies a large corner at the far end of Copacabana and combines bar, restaurant, pizzeria and night spot. Screens, counter, many tables and permanent lighting make it lively rather than intimate. Photos show a place for watching football and having longer meals near Posto 6. Piano adds another layer when scheduled. Do not expect silence.

History and concept

The venue built its identity on extended service and a menu covering breakfast, lunch, pizza, dinner and late nights. It is at 1309 Nossa Senhora de Copacabana, at Júlio de Castilhos, connecting Copacabana, Arpoador and Ipanema. Besides dining it hosts music and celebrations. The mix of feijoada, meat, juices, screens and bar energy explains its appeal.

Public profiles advertise piano-bar evenings Wednesday through Sunday. Events, hours and cover charges may change; confirm with the venue.

My personal take as Ernestinho

I would choose it when a group wants to stay together despite different preferences: pizza for some, Brazilian food for others and a drink while watching football for someone else. Compared with Stalos, its bar atmosphere and proximity to Arpoador stand out, though noise may be a drawback. It is not a one-cuisine specialist; it offers continuity and a lively location. Check music and sports schedules if conversation matters.

What to order and why

Feijoada is frequently mentioned but portions may be large; ask how many people it serves and what sides are included. Pizza works for a late-night table and is easy to share. Meat, sandwiches and juices also appear in accounts, but you do not need to try every category at once. On music nights, begin with a drink and starter. Prices in photos and reviews age quickly: follow the current menu.

Experience by time of day

At breakfast Eclipse acts like a neighborhood café; lunch brings full meals. Afternoon is a break after Arpoador. At dinner and overnight, screens, music and groups change the pace. The venue has been described as open 24 hours, but piano is not constant: evening programs have been advertised Wednesday–Sunday, subject to confirmation. For peaceful feijoada I would go by day; for bar life, after 8 p.m.

My specific advice

Before choosing a table I would look at the screens and ask about piano. For conversation, sit away from the TV; for a match, face it. Late at night, order one round of food and check the bill before calling a car. Keep phone and wallet away from the open frontage in this busy corner.

Eclipse is both a restaurant and a Copacabana meeting place. Choose seating based on whether you want conversation or to follow the venue's events and atmosphere. Ask about the current menu and live-music terms before sitting.

What to combine nearby

I would visit Arpoador before sunset, walk along Francisco Bhering and head to Eclipse afterwards when daylight is no longer essential. For a historic outing, Forte de Copacabana is better in the morning before lunch. For football, arrive early to pick a screen and keep the neighborhood as the rest of the plan.

In Copacabana, Eclipse combines easily with a beachfront walk or night out. If arriving after another activity, confirm the kitchen still operates; an open dining room does not guarantee the full menu.

Directions and map

Eclipse is at Avenida Nossa Senhora de Copacabana, 1309, on the corner of Rua Júlio de Castilhos. General Osório is the closest station; take the exit toward Sá Ferreira and follow Júlio de Castilhos. It’s a short, level walk. You can also walk from Forte de Copacabana via Posto 6. The metro doesn’t run in the early hours, so request a car from inside the venue and verify the plate and driver.
