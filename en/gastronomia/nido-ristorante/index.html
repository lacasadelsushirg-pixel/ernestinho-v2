Nido Ristorante

Ernestinho Carioca · Leblon

LeblonItalian cuisine

Identity and atmosphere

Nido Ristorante at 1011 Avenida General San Martin offers Italian dining designed for an unhurried meal. The restaurant explains that 'Nido' means nest and connects the name with a welcoming atmosphere. I would suggest it for a special occasion, a planned dinner or a relaxed Leblon lunch, not a quick or budget snack stop.

History and concept

The official menu is organized into antipasti, polenta, risotto, fresh and dry pasta, fish, shellfish, meat, sides and desserts. You can build a meal to suit your appetite. I would share a starter and choose fresh pasta or risotto afterwards; fish and meat lovers have other routes. The benefit is making your own sequence rather than relying on a single famous dish.

My personal take as Ernestinho

Riotur has previously featured mushroom-and-truffle ravioli with mascarpone and Parma ham, fresh lasagna with veal ragù and lamb with risotto. These illustrate the style, not today's guaranteed menu. For earthy flavors, ask about mushroom pasta; for a classic, check the current ragù. Couples can order different dishes and save room for dessert.

What to order and why

Wine can be an important part of the experience. Nido says its wine selection can be discussed with staff, who may suggest pairings. I would ask for advice based on the dish rather than choose only by a familiar label. For non-drinkers, ask about alcohol-free drinks. Do not quote vintages or prices without a current list. For allergies, gluten or vegetarian requirements, confirm possible adaptations ahead.

Experience by time of day

Recent reviews praise the setting, cooking and service, though some diners find the bill high even at lunch. I would describe Nido as a mid-to-high-budget outing and check prices before booking, without calling it absolutely expensive or cheap. Starters, mains, wine, desserts and service all affect the total.

My specific advice

For Friday, Saturday or a celebration, I would book. With friends, shared starters and different mains let everyone try more. With children, ask about serving sizes and simpler dishes. This is a slower-paced meal, so avoid an immediate commitment afterwards. Dinner lets you linger in the dining room, while lunch fits more easily into a Leblon outing.

At Nido Ristorante, friends might share a starter and then choose different main courses. Ask which pastas and specialties are available that day, since older recommendations may refer to seasonal menus.

Getting there

The address is 1011 Avenida General San Martin, Leblon. Antero de Quental metro station is a useful landmark, though the route depends on the exit. I would combine it with a neighborhood or waterfront walk, not another meal. Taxis or ride-hailing help when coming from farther away. Confirm hours and booking through official channels.

What to combine nearby

My recommendation is to go when you’re in the mood for a sit-down Italian meal with pasta, risotto and the option of wine. Ask which seasonal dish best represents the restaurant and follow the current menu. Nido is best for a planned, welcoming meal; the potential drawback for travelers is a higher budget and a changing menu, so confirm the menu, hours and reservation.

For a special occasion, confirm your reservation and kitchen hours before setting out. Leave time to enjoy the Italian meal at an easy pace and pair it with a walk around the neighborhood rather than stacking tightly timed activities.
