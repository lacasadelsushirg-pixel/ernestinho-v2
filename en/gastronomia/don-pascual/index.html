Don Pascual

Restaurant, pizzeria, and a food escape in Vargem Grande.

Ernestinho GuideVargem Grande

Identity and visual atmosphere

Don Pascual operates at 867 Estrada do Sacarrão, House 12, Vargem Grande, combining restaurant, pizzeria, lodging and experiences. I would suggest it as a West Zone destination, not a spontaneous stop from Copacabana. Green surroundings and distance call for a reservation, a map and transport both ways; logistics are part of the visit.

History and concept

The official menu consulted in October 2026 was extensive: starters, grilled items, classics, sharing dishes, pizzas, desserts, drinks and set lunches. Listed dishes included octopus salad, house-style lamb, salmon Wellington, rib ravioli, grilled octopus and beef Wellington. Follow the current menu rather than old reviews, as seasonal choices are also advertised.

My personal take as Ernestinho

For two people, shared starters and grilled dishes can offer variety without overordering. For groups, sharing platters and pizzas simplify choices. The menu also lists children's options. Before ordering, confirm weights, sides and seasonal availability; the broad selection makes checking the menu worthwhile.

What to order and why

The pizzeria offers a simpler way to dine; a 28 cm pizza may work for sharing depending on appetite. A consulted menu featured dulce de leche pancakes, banoffee, tiramisu, banana brûlée, chocolate sphere and Bruma de Cupuaçu. Do not treat every dessert as permanently available; check the menu and save room after the main course.

Experience by time of day

The menu also advertised a weekday set lunch from noon to 5 p.m. and wine-pairing menus. Prices consulted in 2026 are dated references, not permanent rates. Spring specials change by definition. Months later, the menu may differ entirely even if the concept remains.

My specific advice

My advice is to book through the official channel and ask about kitchen hours, parking, access and lodging-related experiences. Do not assume day use, breakfast, activities or accommodation are included with a meal. For a full outing, coordinate everything; for dining, confirm the table and transport. Plan the return in this area far from the metro.

At Don Pascual, confirm the dining format because some services may relate to its lodging operation. For a special occasion, ask about reservations, accessibility and dietary restrictions before traveling.

What to combine nearby

I would combine Don Pascual with a day in Vargem Grande and nearby nature, not a tight itinerary across town. Check traffic from other areas. It can be a special lunch for couples, a shared grill-and-pizza meal with friends or a varied family option. Choose it when travel is part of the outing and there is time to linger.

The visit requires more planning than a spontaneous Copacabana meal. Allow travel time and treat it as part of a local outing rather than assume continuous restaurant service.
