Shiso

Japanese hotel cuisine, sake and a minimalist dining room overlooking Barra.

Ernestinho GuideBarra da Tijuca

Identity and atmosphere

Shiso uses a minimalist aesthetic, with wood, clean lines and a calm dining room. Photos show sushi, Japanese dishes and an intimate atmosphere inside the resort, away from the busy avenue.

History and concept

Inside Grand Hyatt Rio, Shiso offers Japanese cuisine, a tasting menu and a sake bar. It combines sushi, sashimi and hot dishes with the structured service of an international hotel.

Conviene confirmar horarios, carta y disponibilidad antes de ir.

My personal take as Ernestinho

I would choose it for a peaceful dinner, especially after a beach day or meetings in Barra. This is not a casual counter: technique, atmosphere and a measured pace of service matter.

What to order and why

I would ask staff to explain the difference between à la carte and tasting menus. For sharing, a sushi and sashimi selection introduces the kitchen without committing the whole evening. Ask about sake and seasonal fish rather than assume the photos show today's menu.

Experience by time of day

Dinner captures the restaurant's identity. Low lighting suits conversation; lunch, when available, may feel more like a hotel meal. Confirm opening days, hours and the length of any tasting menu.

My specific advice

I would book ahead and communicate allergies or preferences before arriving. I would choose an indoor table in uncertain weather and avoid scheduling an immediate transfer downtown after the meal.

For a meal at Shiso, check whether tasting and à la carte menus are offered on your date. Tell the team about allergies when booking, and confirm the cost of drink pairings before deciding.

What to combine nearby

I would combine it with Reserva Beach before dinner and leave the evening open afterward. On rainy days, Grand Hyatt itself can serve as the base for the outing.

A dining experience of this kind deserves an advance reservation. Plan arrival and departure around the confirmed location and allow enough time for every course.
