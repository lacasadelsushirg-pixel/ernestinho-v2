Nino Cucina Ipanema

A small Italian embassy on Barão da Torre, with pasta, wine and a dining room that turns dinner into a journey.

ItalianPastaWinesDinnerRomanticPremium

Identity and atmosphere

Nino Cucina embraces the feel of an Italian villa in the middle of Ipanema: a carefully arranged dining room, closely spaced tables, warm lighting and an atmosphere made for pasta and wine. The photographs show a restaurant that wants to be remembered for the whole experience, beyond a photograph of spaghetti. The room has both a social and a romantic side; its mood changes with your table and the time of day.

History and concept

Nino Cucina began in São Paulo and came to Ipanema with Italian flavours, hospitality and a wine list. Chef Marco Renzetti, associated with Italian fine dining, leads its current phase according to the restaurant's official communications. The address at Barão da Torre, 490, places it on a street of restaurants and makes a visit feel part of Ipanema's dining scene.

Reservations and hours may run in seatings; confirm availability and the current menu.

My personal take as Ernestinho

I would book it for travellers who want an Italian evening without leaving Rio and value the atmosphere as much as the cooking. Nino feels more theatrical and celebratory than an everyday trattoria. That is its strength, although it also raises expectations and the bill. I would choose it for a day when dinner deserves to be remembered rather than a quick meal after the beach.

What to order and why

Spaghetti alla amatriciana appears repeatedly in public references and is a good starting point for pasta with character. The menu for two may be tempting, but I would ask exactly what it includes before choosing: some reviews suggest that not every dish meets expectations. To share, I would order a starter and leave room for pasta, pairing it with a glass suggested by the sommelier rather than choosing by the label alone.

Experience by time of day

Lunch sittings offer a way to experience the restaurant in daylight with less ceremony. Dinner brings the full Italian experience, especially around 7 p.m., when the dining room begins to fill. Sunday has shorter hours, and I would not treat it as a late-night restaurant even though it closes late on some days. Booking a sitting helps organise the visit and avoids arriving expecting an immediate table.

My specific advice

I would book the first dinner sitting if you want to walk around Ipanema afterwards; you can eat without pressure and see the room change. I would ask which pasta is most recommended that day and how long it takes to prepare. Ordering too many starters before the pasta would be a mistake: at Nino, the main course deserves room of its own.

At Nino Cucina Ipanema, check the pasta dishes and seasonal menu before deciding. If sharing starters, ask about portion sizes and leave room for the main course. Prices and preparations may change.

What to combine nearby

I would visit Arpoador before an early dinner and walk to Nino. Afterward, a short stroll along Garcia D’Ávila is enough. At lunch, Praça Nossa Senhora da Paz can be the next stop.

The visit fits well into an evening dining in Ipanema. I would book ahead for a specific time and allow room for a walk around the neighbourhood before or afterwards without rushing the meal.
