RIO GUIDE

Arrive at Rio: Galeão and Santos Dumont

Rio de Janeiro has two main airports. Always check your flight code: GIG and SDU are in different areas and the transfer time can change a lot.

Ernestinho Council

Send us the flight number, not just the time. So we can follow delays or changes and properly coordinate your reception.

01

GIG – Galeão International Airport

It is in Ilha do Governador and concentrates most of the international flights. For Copacabana, Ipanema, Centro or Barra you should consider distance, transit, hours and luggage.

02

SDU – Santos Dumont

It is next to the Center and near the South Zone. Although it seems very close on the map, transit and access during events can increase the time.

03

Taxi

There are taxis and official services. It confirms mode and price before leaving; as in many tourist destinations, improvised hiring may be more expensive.

04

Applications

Uber and 99 have meeting points or procedures that can vary by terminal. Check registration and driver before climbing.

05

Public transport

It may be economical, but it is not always practical with children, many suitcases, night arrival or several connections. Check route and billeting before starting.

06

Ernestinho transfer

If you prefer to hire a vehicle with a driver, see the private transfer and travel quote.

A team member welcomes you at the airport, accompanies you to the vehicle and our drivers take you to the destination. We have alternatives for couples, families and groups.

07

You pay when you get there.

You do not need to pay in advance: pay when you are in destination, according to the conditions confirmed when booking. Send date, flight, destination, passengers and baggage to quote.

08

You're gonna take a car back when you get there?

If your trip continues outside Rio, compare removing the vehicle directly in GIG or SDU. If you will spend several days in the city first, you can agree to rent it only from the day of departure. You will find the comparator and all the tips in Guide to Rio → Car rental in Rio and Brazil.
