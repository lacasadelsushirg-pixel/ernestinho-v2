RIO GUIDE · Suitcases and luggage storage

Where to keep your bags in Rio de Janeiro

Your room is delivered in the morning, but your trip doesn't end there. Before you go out with your suitcase behind, decide where to leave it and how to remove it: that changes what you can do in Rio during those hours. This guide helps you choose between checking your accommodation and booking a luggage storage service.

01

When the suitcases slow you down the day

The most common case is early check-out and night flight, but also an arrival before check-in, change of department, stopover, departure for Novo Rio or an activity before the trip. Not all situations are solved the same: a suitcase for a few hours does not require the same logistics as several bags during a change of city. Write down where you leave and where you go afterwards.

02

First ask in your accommodation

Ask if they can receive luggage before check-in or keep it after check-out, until what time and under what conditions. A hotel may have reception; a department may require coordination with another person. Do not assume that the service exists, that it is free or that you can return whenever you want. Ask for a clear answer before organizing the last day.

03

When a luggage storage is suitable

A reserved service can be useful if the accommodation does not keep suitcases, is outside your route or does not allow them to be removed at the required time. Compare the expense and the complete detour, not only the distance on the map. The best location is the one that fits your departure: leaving the suitcase on the other side of Rio can eat the time you wanted to earn.

05

Checklist before paying

Check for full address, luggage access, delivery and withdrawal times, quantity and type of bags, total value and modification or cancellation conditions. If you have a special piece, check if that point accepts it. Add the pick-up time to the next transfer. A location near a station does not mean that it is inside it or that it remains open overnight.

06

Copacabana and Ipanema: choose your route

If these hours are spent in the South Zone, look around where you will really be. Copacabana is extensive: an address close to the hotel can be more practical than another more famous one. In Ipanema, think about your walk and the subsequent exit. Check out the updated supplier map; this guide does not promise a specific venue available for any date.

07

Transit centres and areas

Center can fit with an urban walk, a cultural visit or an exit to SDU, but check the return with luggage and the actual schedules. If your plan ends in another area, do not choose only for proximity to the first attraction. Port, stations and terminals have their own accesses: confirm the exact point and avoid assuming that a nearby service works within the terminal.

08

GIG and SDU: calculated from withdrawal

Check your flight code: Galeão (GIG) and Santos Dumont (SDU) are not interchangeable. The account starts where you pick up the suitcase and includes waiting, loading, transfer and flight procedures. Do not use the proximity of SDU to the Center as permission to leave the limit. For GIG, organize the actual journey from your final location and leave room according to traffic and recommendations of your airline.

09

Novo Rio and Puerto: don't confuse the address

If you leave by bus, confirm terminal, company and boarding point on your ticket. If you arrive or depart by cruise, follow the instructions of the shipping company. A luggage keeper can resolve waiting hours, but does not replace the boarding times. We do not take a confirmed service within Novo Rio or the port: check each option in your channel and calculate the withdrawal before moving.

10

What to do with those hours off

Choose a proportionate plan: a walk along the coast, coffee, lunch or a cultural visit near the chosen area. Avoid adding a distant attraction just because now you do not carry luggage. An activity with entry or schedule needs its own margin. If you have a transfer hired, inform where you will remove your bags and agree to the meeting point; do not assume an additional stop included.

11

Rio + Búzios: think of the next stage

If you change the city, arrange the luggage as part of the transfer. Saving it in Rio only makes sense if you return to look for it within the agreed schedule. For a stay in Búzios, also ask the accommodation for luggage reception. We do not claim that Bounce operates at any point in Búzios: this recommendation refers to a real connection by Rio.

12

Safety and common sense

Take with you documents, money, necessary medication and what you will use during the day. Check the terms on accepted items and keep vouchers and contact instructions. When removing, check your parts before leaving. Any protection, liability or claim depends on the conditions of the provider; Ernestinho does not add a warranty of its own.

Can I go directly without reservation?
